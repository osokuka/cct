Shift Guidelines

Naming Conventions

  • Use descriptive names (e.g., "Morning Shift", "Afternoon Shift")
  • Avoid generic names like "Shift 1" or "Shift A"
  • Consider including time ranges in the name

Time Management

  • Use 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00, 14:30, 22:00)
  • Overnight shifts are supported (end time before start time)
  • Typical shift duration is 8 hours

Best Practices

  • Create shifts that don't overlap unnecessarily
  • Consider camp-specific requirements
  • Deactivate unused shifts to keep the list clean
